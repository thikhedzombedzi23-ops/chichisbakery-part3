Welcome to Chichi's Bakery

Chichi's Bakery began as a home-baking hobby and has grown into a beloved neighbourhood bakery. Every loaf, cake and pastry is made by hand using traditional techniques and locally sourced ingredients, bringing warmth and quality to our community every single day.

What We Bake

  • Artisan Bread

    Sourdough, rye and wholewheat loaves baked fresh every morning.

  • Celebration Cakes

    Custom cakes for birthdays, weddings and special occasions.

  • Pastries

    Croissants, muffins and hand pies baked fresh daily.
