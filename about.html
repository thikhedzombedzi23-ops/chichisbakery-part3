About Chichi's Bakery

Our History

Chichi's Bakery was founded in 2015 by Chichi Nkosi, who began baking bread for neighbours from her home kitchen. Word of the quality and care behind every loaf spread quickly, and within two years Chichi opened her first small shopfront. Today the bakery employs a team of four and is known throughout the neighbourhood for its sourdough, celebration cakes and daily-fresh pastries.

Our Mission

To bring warm, handmade, high-quality baked goods to our community using traditional techniques and locally sourced ingredients.

Our Vision

To become the neighbourhood's most loved bakery, recognised for consistency, freshness and personal service.
